Who we are

Unspace ("the app", "we", "us") is published by Zintex OÜ, a company registered in Estonia. Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Zintex OÜ is the data controller for personal data processed through the app.

Privacy questions: unspaceapp@gmail.com.

What we collect

All of the data below is linked to your account.

  • Account data — email address, username, display name, date of birth, password (stored hashed, never in plain text)
  • Profile data — avatar, bio, themes, fonts, stickers, profile music, widgets, badges, follows and followers
  • Content you publish — posts, Diary entries, comments, wall messages, photos, videos, voice notes and reactions
  • Messages — direct and group messages you send, their attachments and read state
  • Push token — the device token Apple issues if you allow notifications
  • Subscription data — an anonymised subscriber ID and purchase history for Unspace Premium
  • Technical data — IP address, device model, OS and app version, and server request logs
  • Safety data — reports you file, accounts you block, and moderation actions on your account

We do not collect precise location, health data, your phone contacts, or your browsing outside the app. We do not ask you for payment card details — purchases are handled entirely by Apple.

Why we process it, and on what legal basis

  • To create and run your account, show your profile and deliver your posts and messages — performance of our contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b))
  • To send push notifications about activity involving you — your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time in iOS Settings
  • To process Unspace Premium — performance of contract (Art. 6(1)(b))
  • To detect and act on abuse, spam, ban evasion and illegal content — our legitimate interest in keeping the platform safe (Art. 6(1)(f))
  • To fix crashes and keep the service working — legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f))
  • To answer legal requests and takedown notices — legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c))

Who else processes your data

We use the following providers, each acting as our processor under GDPR Art. 28:

  • Supabase — authentication, database and file storage. Data is hosted in the European Union.
  • Cloudflare R2 — storage and delivery of photos, videos and other media you upload.
  • RevenueCat — management of Unspace Premium subscriptions (United States).
  • Apple — app distribution, In-App Purchase and push notification delivery.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with data brokers. Where a provider processes data outside the EU/EEA, the transfer is covered by the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

If we add advertising or attribution technology to the app in future, we will update this page before that version is released, and declare it on the App Store privacy label.

How long we keep it

  • Account, profile, posts and messages — until you delete your account.
  • Server logs — up to 30 days, then deleted.
  • Reports and moderation records — at least one year after the account is closed, so that repeat abuse can be recognised.
  • Banned accounts — kept in a deactivated state to prevent the same person from immediately returning.
  • Purchase records — for the period Estonian accounting law requires (7 years).

When you delete your account we remove or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except anything we are legally required to keep.

Security

  • All traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS with TLS 1.2 or higher.
  • Passwords are hashed; we never see or store them in readable form.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain.
  • Access to production data is limited to the people who need it, behind strong authentication.
  • Database access rules restrict every account to the data it is allowed to see.

No system is completely secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate where the GDPR requires it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the data we hold about you (access and portability)
  • correct data that is wrong or incomplete
  • delete your data — you can do this yourself in the app under Settings → Delete account
  • restrict or object to processing we base on legitimate interests
  • withdraw consent you have given, such as for push notifications

Write to unspaceapp@gmail.com and we will respond within one month. You also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority, or to ours: Andmekaitse Inspektsioon, Tatari 39, 10134 Tallinn, Estonia — aki.ee.

Age

Unspace is intended for users aged 17 and over, matching the app’s App Store rating. We ask for your date of birth at sign-up. If we learn that a younger child has created an account, we remove it and the data with it. See also our child safety standards.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced in the app or by email, and the date at the top of this page always shows the current version.
